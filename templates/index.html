Core Waste Categories

Source segregation ensures materials are recycled and processed according to municipal guidelines:

1. Wet Waste

Green Bin

Biodegradable kitchen and garden waste that decomposes naturally into nutrient-rich compost.

Examples: Fruit peels, leftover food, tea leaves.

2. Dry Waste

Blue Bin

Non-biodegradable, non-recyclable solid waste that cannot be reprocessed mechanically.

Examples: Soiled paper, wrappers, cardboard.

3. Recyclable Waste

Yellow Bin

Clean materials that can be reprocessed into secondary raw materials for new goods.

Examples: PET plastic bottles, drink cans, glasses.

4. E-Waste

Red Bin

Hazardous electronic products containing heavy metals requiring specialized recovery.

Examples: Phone chargers, USB cables, batteries, PCBs.

What is Waste Segregation and Why is it Important?

Waste segregation is the practice of dividing solid waste into distinct categories at the point of generation (households, schools, offices) before collection and disposal.

🌍 Reduces Landfill Burden

Over 80% of municipal solid waste can be composted or recycled when segregated, drastically minimizing landfill space and toxic leachate.

🔄 Powers Circular Economy

Clean, uncontaminated recyclables (metals, plastics, glass) are returned directly to manufacturers, saving raw materials and energy.

🛡️ Protects Sanitation Workers

Segregating hazardous items (broken glass, needles, e-waste) prevents serious occupational injuries for waste handlers and informal recyclers.
